## 1. In this repository

### OpenXR headers and registry

`packages/rust/openxr-abi/src/bindings.rs` is generated by bindgen from the OpenXR headers
(`openxr.h`, `openxr_platform.h`, `openxr_loader_negotiation.h`).
`packages/rust/runtime/src/generated.rs` is generated from the OpenXR registry (`xr.xml`).
Both come from OpenXR-SDK-Source release-1.1.65.

    Copyright (c) 2017-2026 The Khronos Group Inc.
    SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0 OR MIT

OpenLoop uses them under the MIT licence (section 4.1).

### OpenXR-SDK-Source (git submodule)

`third_party/OpenXR-SDK-Source` points to
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/OpenXR-SDK-Source (release-1.1.65). Its files are not part
of this repository. Once fetched, they are under the licences in that project's `LICENSE`
file and `LICENSES/` directory.
